About

Minnesota's Redneck Rock N Roll, based in the Twin Cities.

Two lead singers. One rock, one country. That's how Whiskey Peddler goes from Metallica to Morgan Wallen without the floor going cold.

Formed in 2023, the band plays bars, backyards and festival stages across Minnesota and Iowa. Every song gets pushed harder and played louder than the original. Full effort, no filler.
