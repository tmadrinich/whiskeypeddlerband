Bio

Whiskey Peddler is a Minnesota-based cover band built around classic country, alt-country, and rock 'n' roll — played louder and rougher than the originals. The band doesn't do polite renditions; every set is high-energy, foot-on-the-monitor rock and roll, whether the room is a neighborhood dive bar or a full festival stage.
