Bio

Whiskey Peddler is a Minnesota-based party band built around classic country, alt-country, and rock 'n' roll — played louder and rougher than the originals. The band doesn't do polite renditions; every set is high-energy, foot-on-the-monitor rock and roll, whether the room is a neighborhood dive bar or a full festival stage.

Why Whiskey Peddler

A set list that moves between country and rock without missing a beat. We read the crowd and adjust in real time — so when the energy would normally dip, we're already onto the next song that keeps people on the floor. If you need a band that holds the room from open to last call, that's what we do.
